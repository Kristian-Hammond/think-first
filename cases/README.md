# Example cases

Five fictional consulting cases for trying out the interventions. The companies, figures and people are invented.

| Case | Topic | Company |
|---|---|---|
| [case-01](case-01/) | Declining Profitability at a Retail Chain | Meridian Home & Living |
| [case-02](case-02/) | Market Entry for a Technology Company | Northstar Workflow |
| [case-03](case-03/) | Customer Attrition at a Financial Services Firm | HarborPoint Financial |
| [case-04](case-04/) | Operational Bottlenecks at a Manufacturing Company | PrecisionCore Components |
| [case-05](case-05/) | Evaluating an AI Investment in a Professional Services Firm | Alder & Gray Advisory |

Each case has four files:

| File | What it is | Where to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `problem-description.txt` | The client problem, in a few sentences | Any "The problem" box |
| `company.txt` | A one-page company brief | Supporting documents (Know your Materials, Explain the Answer, Spot the Flaw, Client Rehearsal) |
| `industry.txt` | A one-page industry brief | Supporting documents, as above |
| `recommended-solution.txt` | A worked recommendation | Any "Your solution" box (Critique, Devil's Advocate, Assumptions Audit, Pre-mortem, Say it Simply, Client Rehearsal) |

The "Load from Word file" buttons and the document uploads accept these `.txt` files directly.
