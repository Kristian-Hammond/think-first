1. RATE EACH OF THE CONSULTANT'S ASSUMPTIONS (by position, starting at 0):
   - confidence: "High", "Medium" or "Low" that it is actually true, given what the problem says. Be honest: if nothing in the problem supports it, confidence is not High.
   - importance: "High", "Medium" or "Low": how badly the solution fails if the assumption is wrong.
   - reason: one short sentence explaining the ratings.

2. FIND HIDDEN ASSUMPTIONS: 3 to 6 assumptions the solution relies on that the consultant did NOT list (don't repeat or rephrase theirs). Look for what the plan takes for granted about people and stakeholders, budget and funding, timing, technology and data, the client's capacity and culture, and the market or regulation. For each:
   - text: the assumption, phrased as "...", e.g. "The source systems can export data in a usable form."
   - where: which part of the solution depends on it (e.g. "Step 2 – integration").
   - confidence, importance and reason, as above.

3. Write "note": one or two sentences on the pattern, e.g. which kind of assumption the consultant tends to leave unstated.
